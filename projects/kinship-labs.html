Product storytelling

Kinship Labs

A narrative and visual system for a product team preparing investor, sales, and launch materials around a new collaborative software tool.

Scope Story, decks, web direction
Industry Software
Role Visual storytelling
Challenge

Kinship had a strong product but a fragmented story. Investor, sales, and launch materials each explained the product differently, making the value proposition harder to remember.

The new system centered the product around shared momentum, pairing crisp interface framing with a flexible deck language and direct messaging structure.

Delivered
  • Narrative framework Messaging hierarchy, proof points, and audience-specific story arcs.
  • Deck system Investor, sales, and product overview templates with reusable modules.
  • Web direction Landing page structure, launch visuals, and conversion-focused sections.
