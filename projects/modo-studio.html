Digital experience

Modo Studio

A modular portfolio and launch microsite system for a spatial design studio that needed to present work with more pace, clarity, and editorial range.

Scope Web design, content system
Industry Architecture and interiors
Role Digital design
Challenge

Modo's previous site buried its strongest work behind inconsistent project pages. The team needed a digital system that could support short announcements, deep case studies, and new-business materials from the same visual language.

The solution pairs calm typography with high-contrast content modules, giving each project enough space to breathe while keeping the browsing experience direct.

Delivered
  • Page templates Home, project index, case study, studio profile, and contact flows.
  • Content model Repeatable modules for imagery, credits, quotes, and project data.
  • Launch kit Social crops, deck slides, and guidance for publishing new work.
