Campaign direction

Frame/Fair

A graphic campaign system for an independent film festival built to move from posters to wayfinding, social, and motion without losing its cinematic edge.

Scope Campaign, print, motion kit
Industry Culture and events
Role Art direction
Challenge

The festival needed a visual language that could announce a broad slate of screenings while still feeling curated and unmistakable. Each format had different information density, production needs, and turnaround constraints.

The final system uses a bold diagonal frame, saturated gradients, and flexible image crops to create instant recognition across printed, digital, and environmental placements.

Delivered
  • Key art Festival poster, title lockups, and adaptable hero compositions.
  • Motion toolkit Graphic rules for trailers, bumpers, and social cutdowns.
  • Venue system Wayfinding, schedule boards, credentials, and sponsor placements.
