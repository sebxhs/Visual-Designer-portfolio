Visual system

Alta Supply

A flexible identity for a materials marketplace bringing suppliers, fabricators, and design teams into one clearer product ecosystem.

Scope Identity, taxonomy, sales tools
Industry Marketplace and materials
Role Brand system design
Challenge

Alta's product range was technical and varied, which made the brand feel fragmented across sales decks, category pages, and partner materials. The new system needed to simplify complexity without flattening the range of materials on offer.

A geometric mark family, practical color logic, and category graphics created a shared language for both product discovery and business development.

Delivered
  • Mark family Core logo, category symbols, partner badges, and usage rules.
  • Taxonomy graphics Visual cues for browsing, comparison, and material education.
  • Sales system Deck templates, one-sheets, and partner presentation assets.
