Brand identity

Northline Coffee Co.

A warm, modular identity for a neighborhood coffee company expanding from one beloved cafe into packaged retail and seasonal releases.

Scope Identity, packaging, voice
Industry Food and beverage
Role Design direction
Challenge

Northline had a loyal cafe following, but its retail packaging and store materials felt disconnected. The team needed a system that could carry everyday coffee bags, limited roasts, menu boards, and social launches without losing the warmth of the original shop.

The identity leans on sturdy typography, sun-washed color, and a graphic seal that adapts across roast levels and seasonal moments.

Delivered
  • Packaging system Roast labels, color rules, and a seasonal drop framework.
  • Brand voice Messaging guidance for bags, menus, emails, and launch posts.
  • Retail kit Signage, loyalty cards, stickers, and production-ready templates.
